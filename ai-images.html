1) Key Principles for Image Generation Prompts

  1. Be Specific About the Subject: Clearly describe the main object(s), characters, or scene.
  2. Include Style and Mood: Specify artistic styles (e.g., watercolor, photorealistic), mood, or era.
  3. Mention Colors and Lighting: Describe color schemes, lighting conditions, or atmosphere.
  4. Add Composition Details: Indicate perspective, framing, background elements, or focal points.
  5. Use Reference Keywords: Include references to known artists, genres, or visual themes.
  6. Avoid Ambiguity: Be descriptive to reduce unexpected results.

5) Tips for Better Image Prompts

  • Use adjectives to enrich descriptions (e.g., “glowing,” “rustic,” “majestic”).
  • Combine multiple elements but keep the prompt concise and clear.
  • Experiment with artistic references to guide style.
  • Specify negative prompts if your tool supports them (e.g., “no text,” “no blur”).
  • Review generated images and refine your prompt iteratively.
